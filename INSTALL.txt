ETLab MITS Reskin - Local Install

This is a client-side browser extension. It only runs on:
https://mits.etlab.app/*

Chrome or Edge install:
1. Open chrome://extensions/ or edge://extensions/
2. Turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked.
4. Select this folder:
   D:\VS\PROJECTS\FREE WORK\etlab-reskin-ext
5. Open https://mits.etlab.app/ and log in normally.

Use:
- Press Alt+K to open universal search.
- Use Up / Down to move through results.
- Press Enter to open a selected result.
- Press Esc to close search.
- Click the floating blue search button (bottom-right) anytime.
- The dashboard gets a unified academic + hostel calendar.

Privacy:
- The extension does not store your username or password.
- It uses your normal ETLab browser session.
- Hostel calendar data is fetched only from ETLab while you are logged in.
